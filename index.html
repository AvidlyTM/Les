About Leslie

Leslie J. Valladares

Leslie J. Valladares is a native Texan, tracing her ancestry back to the first 300 settlers to Texas. Having grown up in the small town of Troy, it is Mrs. Valladares's goal to provide legal services to this historically underserved community. Leslie graduated from Troy High School, Temple College, and Tarleton State University. Before becoming an attorney, she built a career in the Bell County legal community, working alongside some of its most respected lawyers, and then set out to realize a personal dream of attending law school. Leslie earned her juris doctorate degree from Mitchell Hamline College of Law in St. Paul, Minnesota. She is licensed by the State Bar of Texas.

Ms. Valladares offers a variety of legal services to her clients, in areas including (but not limited to) family law, estate planning, business law, criminal law, and equine law matters. She is a member of the Texas Criminal Defense Lawyers Association. Leslie is a legal generalist and is not licensed by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.
